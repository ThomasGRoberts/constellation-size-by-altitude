Methods & limits

N-fold coverage means at least n satellites are simultaneously above 10° elevation at each sampled ground site. The service latitude control sets the region from its southern to northern bound; the search compares inclinations from 30° to 90° and selects the lowest-cost Walker design among its candidates. Sites are tested every 5° latitude and 15° longitude, every two hours over one day. These sampled results do not guarantee continuous coverage. The 2D map shows counts across the whole Earth; dashed lines mark the selected service region.

The minimum elevation angle is fixed at 10°. Choosing the fewest planes may use up to 25% more satellites than choosing the fewest satellites.
